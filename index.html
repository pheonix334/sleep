Sleep Facts

Recommended sleep by age

Age groupRecommended
Teens (14–17)8–10 hrs
Young adults (18–25)7–9 hrs
Adults (26–64)7–9 hrs
Older adults (65+)7–8 hrs
Sleep isn't uniform through the night — it moves through light sleep, deep sleep, and REM in a repeating pattern that commonly runs about 90 minutes per cycle. Waking up in the middle of deep sleep tends to feel groggy, while waking near the end of a cycle, as it shifts back toward lighter sleep, tends to feel more refreshed. That's why these calculators work in full-cycle increments rather than picking an exact hour count.

About Healthy Life Scale

Healthy Life Scale is a collection of free health and wellness calculators — a calorie and macro tracker, BMI calculator, water intake calculator, TDEE calculator, sleep calculator, pregnancy due date and weight gain calculators, period and ovulation tracker, child height predictor, blood pressure calculator, heart age calculator, and breath holding test. Every tool is free to use, works without an account or app download, and saves your data privately on your own device rather than a server. Built for anyone who wants quick, practical health calculators without ads, logins, or paywalls.
